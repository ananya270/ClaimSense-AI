Verification Pipeline Architecture

10 Stages
01

Claim Submission

Upload policy, claim form, bills, vehicle photos & description

02

Document OCR

Text extraction from multi-format files (PDF, images, DOCX)

03

Information Extraction

Regex & structured parsing of policy, amounts, dates & vehicle #

04

Computer Vision

Pretrained vehicle damage & component location detection

05

NLP Analysis

Extraction of accident chronology and stated damage locations

06

Cross-Verification

Rule-based consistency engine comparing doc vs. image vs. statement

07

Risk Assessment

Weighted scoring producing Low / Medium / High risk levels

08

LLM Explanation

Transparent summary explaining risk triggers for human review

09

Verification Report

Structured assessor report with evidence breakdowns
